1. Machines, in priority order

Save file: %USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\Questing Goose Studio\Probably Stolen\save_0.es3. Higher machines get first pick of modules. A target is a minimum (or a job deadline); after every target is met, each machine maximises its stats in the order you choose. A job deadline is a stop point: Performance stops at what the deadline needs, and spare modules go to the other machines. A machine whose installed modules already meet its target is kept exactly as it is. Imported machines start with targets that keep what they do now, and machines with a job deadline do not maximise further; change either per machine.
