Settings

Growth per night is 8 at base (Hydroponic System: Performance/2 speeds it up). Hydroponic Quality/2 is % more harvest. DNA Quality/2 adds to every edit's success chance.

DNA Manipulator modules and seeds you own

Precision Extraction picks the exact trait to extract, once you use it on a disk of that trait to copy it (so the first disk of each trait comes by luck). Without it, extraction picks a random trait (a Positive or Negative Trait Filter narrows it to good or bad traits). Genetic Stabilizer keeps the seed alive when you extract, which is the only way to remove a trait.

Traits you can get

Ticked by default: every trait some plant has naturally, so you can extract it onto a disk. Simple/Complex Genome are locked in the game and cannot be moved.
