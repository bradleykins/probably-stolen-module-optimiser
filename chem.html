Modules are Chemical Equipment Modules (0-4). They cut Blender loss (35/20/10/5/0%), speed up the Heating Plate (×4 at 3, ×16 at 4) and cut Finisher loss (12/6/3/1/0%). The Finisher swaps its loss for contaminant, so Lab Pure (99%) needs 3+ Finisher modules. Shop bottles carry 2-8% random impurity; recipes use the 5% average.

Not here: Zyanide and Birth Control Pills (no recipe in the game; their purity comes from the bought item).
