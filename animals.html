Fish (RAS tank)

A fish turns a fixed share of every kcal it eats into meat (its conversion), at any age, so age only changes how that meat is cut and how much tank it uses. Bigger fillets are worth more per kcal. Value counts meat, Skin Grafts and Fish Scrap at their sale value, less what the fish cost to buy. Ages are nights in the tank while fed; from its old age (red) each night has a 50% chance it dies.

Rats (Animal Cage)

A rat eats its Hunger Rate every night whatever its size, and its meat is its weight cut into 400 g Raw Meat, 200 g Small Raw Meat, 100 g Morsels and a Small Morsel for every 50 g started. It grows fast to day 5, slowly to day 10, then loses weight from day 11. From day 14 (× Longevity) each night has a 75% chance it dies. Rats breed from day 3.
